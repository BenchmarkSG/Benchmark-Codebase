WHAT "MISSING" MEANS HERE
  A rate counts as HAVE only if it is SOURCED — is_indicative = false in
  product_rates. The app serves only sourced rows (RATES_REAL_ONLY defaults
  true), so a generated / indicative row is not a rate we have. Most lines
  below do have an indicative row sitting behind them. It is not served, and
  it must never be published as a market rate.

HOW TO FILL IT IN
  Write the rate after the "=", the way the bank publishes it:
      6.25            a plain figure
      8.00-9.50       a published range  (stored as the midpoint, shown ~8.75%)
      from 7.5        a published floor
      n/a             the bank does not offer this
  Put the source URL after the "#" if you have it. Blank lines are skipped on
  import, so a partly filled file is fine — send it back whenever.

  Every figure must come off the bank's own published rate sheet. Do not
  estimate, interpolate, or copy one from a comparison site.
