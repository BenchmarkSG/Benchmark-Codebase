# Bank logo provenance

Where each logo in `client/public/images/bankLogos/` came from, and under what
licence. Added 2026-09-16, first for the 20 banks that previously rendered as SVG
text wordmarks in the "Banks & Companies We Track" carousel, then for a further 13
when the carousel was aligned to `public.banks` so that every benchmarked bank
shows its real logo.

All were downloaded from the English Wikipedia article for the bank, as a 480px
PNG rasterisation of the source file. Each was checked by eye against the bank it
is filed under before being installed.

> **Read the licence column before launch.** The site shows bank logos for
> identification only (see the footer disclaimer), but the rows marked *Fair use*
> are non-free files hosted under a rationale that covers **Wikipedia**, not this
> site. The CC BY-SA row carries an attribution requirement.
