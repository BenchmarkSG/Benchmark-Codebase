# FeeOptima — Sales Pitch Script

**Setting:** The interviewer is playing a business owner. Your job is to sell FeeOptima
so well that they don't just buy it once, but make it *the* thing they check before
every banking decision, for good.

**Length:** about 6–8 minutes if nobody interrupts. Each section says roughly how long
to spend on it. Lines in *italics* are stage directions, not things to say.

**The one idea to keep coming back to:**
> "Your bank knows exactly what it charges everyone else. You don't. We fix that, and we
> stay on your side every time you deal with a bank from now on."

## 1. The Hook (30 seconds)

*Lean in. Ask a question before you pitch anything.*

> "Before I tell you what we do, one quick question. What interest rate is your business
> paying on its overdraft, or its loan, right now?"

*Let them answer. Most people know it roughly.*

> "Great. Now the harder one: **is that a good rate?** Compared with what the bank down
> the road would give you for the same thing?"

*Pause. They almost never know.*

> "That's the problem. Nobody knows, and the banks count on it."

---

## 2. The Problem (1 minute)

> "Banking is the only big cost in your business you can't compare. Think about it:
>
> - **FX costs are hidden.** When you send money abroad, the bank doesn't show a fee.
>   It quietly gives you a worse exchange rate. You never see the charge, but you pay it.
> - **Loan pricing is private.** Two businesses of the same size at the same bank can
>   pay very different rates. It comes down to who asked.
> - **Fees are split up.** A single international transfer can have three separate
>   charges: the sending bank, the middleman bank, and the receiving bank.
>
> So people stay put and keep paying. Not because the deal is good, but because checking
> is too hard.
>
> Here's the part that matters to you: **it adds up every year.** Say a business sends
> S$250,000 a month overseas and pays a 1.8% hidden FX margin, when 0.35% is on offer
> elsewhere. That's about **S$43,000 a year**, gone. Next year it happens again."

*Tip: if they gave you a real number in Section 1, use it here. A number about their own
business beats any example.*

---

## 3. The Solution (1 minute)

> "FeeOptima is an independent benchmark for your banking costs.
>
> You type in what you pay: your loan rate, your deposit rate, or what you sent and what
> arrived on a transfer. **In seconds** we compare it with real published rates from the
> other banks in your market and tell you three things:
>
> 1. **Are you overpaying?** Yes or no.
> 2. **By how much, in dollars per year.** Not basis points, not jargon. Money.
> 3. **What the best rate available actually is.**
>
> No statements to upload, no bank login, no sign-up needed to see your number. You
> give us a few figures and we give you the answer."

*If you can, open the site on your phone and run one live. A real result on screen
sells better than anything you say.*

> "Four steps:

## 5. Key Features (1.5 minutes)

*Don't read all of these. Pick the 3–4 that fit the person in front of you.*

### For the business owner who cares about money
- **Savings in dollars per year**, sized to *your* loan, deposit or transfer volume.
- **Costs almost nothing to find out.** The benchmark is free. The reveal costs about as
  much as a coffee. Even our top tier is S$199, and one successful negotiation on a
  business loan can pay that back many times over.

### For the one who cares about trust
- **We are independent.** We don't hold your money, don't sell you loans, and aren't
  owned by a bank. Our only job is to find you a better deal.
- **Real rates only.** Every rate comes from a published source: central banks like
  the Bank of Thailand and Bank Negara Malaysia, banks' own rate pages, and live FX
  data. If we don't have real data for something, we say *"no benchmark yet"* rather
  than make up a number. We'd rather show you nothing than show you something wrong.
- **Privacy first.** You type numbers; you don't upload statements or connect your bank
  account.
- **Secure payments** through Stripe.

### For the busy one
- **Answer in seconds**, before they've finished their coffee.
- **Option 3 takes it off their plate completely.** An expert does the talking.
- **Book a call in-app**, with WhatsApp as the main way we reach you, and a calendar
  invite straight to your phone.

### For the one who runs across borders
- **5 markets, one tool:** Singapore, India, Malaysia, Indonesia, Thailand.
- **Cross-border transfers priced against the live mid-market exchange rate**, so you
  see the *real* all-in cost of every payment, hidden margin included.
- **Covers both sides of the business:** the owner's personal mortgage and car loan, and
  the company's overdraft, deposits, FX and trade facilities.

### Extras worth a mention
- **Personal dashboard.** Your benchmarks are saved, so you can reopen them and act later.
- **Treasury & operational efficiency checks.** A quick questionnaire that scores how
  well your business manages cash, from 0 to 100, and tells you if an expert is worth
  talking to.
- **A shareable certificate** when you've benchmarked. A nice way to show your
  CFO or partner.

## 7. Handling Objections

*They will push back; that's the test. Stay calm, agree first, then answer.*

**"I have a good relationship with my bank. They look after me."**
> "That's great, and we're not asking you to leave them. Most of our customers *stay*
> with their bank and just use our number to get a better rate from it. A good
> relationship should survive a fair question, and if it's truly the best deal, we'll
> tell you that too. You'd get peace of mind for free."

**"Why don't I just Google the rates myself?"**
> "You can, for about 60 banks across 5 countries, product by product, with each one
> reporting rates differently. For FX you'd also have to work out the hidden margin
> yourself against the mid-market rate. We've done that work, we keep it up to date
> every day, and we turn it into one number in dollars."

**"How do I know your numbers are right?"**
> "Every rate comes from a published source: central banks, banks' own pages, live FX
> data. We never guess. If we don't have real data for a product in your market, we tell
> you so instead of making up a figure. That's a rule we don't break."

**"Sounds like a comparison site. There are plenty already."**
> "Comparison sites show you *advertised* rates for new customers and get paid by the
> banks they list. We start from *your* current rate, show you *your* gap in dollars, and
> then help you close it: an email, a coach, or an ex-banker negotiating for you. Nobody
> else does that last part."

**"Why should I pay S$199?"**
> "You don't have to. The benchmark and the negotiation email are free. S$199 is for
> when your time is worth more than doing it yourself. On a S$500,000 facility, getting
> just 0.25% off is S$1,250 a year, every year. S$199 is small by comparison."

**"What about my data?"**
> "You type in numbers. No bank login, no statement upload. Payments go through Stripe;
> we never see your card. We're a software platform, not a bank or a broker."

**"What if you don't find me any savings?"**
> "Then you've spent nothing, because finding out is free, and you'll know for sure that
> you're getting a good deal. That's worth knowing too."

## 10. Don't overclaim

If the interviewer knows the product, these will come up. Be honest; it builds trust.

- **Coverage is still growing.** Real per-bank data is live for many product/market
  combinations, not all. Trade finance and LC/guarantee pricing aren't published by
  anyone, so those will come from our advisors' real deal records over time. Say:
  *"Where there's no public data, we show nothing rather than guess."*
- **Cross-border savings figures are being recalibrated** so the customer's cost and the
  banks' costs are measured against the same mid-market rate. Don't quote a specific
  cross-border saving as final.
- **Don't promise a guaranteed saving.** Results are informational, not financial
  advice. Say "could save", never "will save".
- **The S$43,000 example is illustrative.** Present it as "for example", not as a
  customer result.
- **Rate-change alerts** on saved benchmarks are shown in account settings. If asked,
  describe them as something being built out rather than a finished feature.
